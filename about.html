About Caelus Air

The Idea Behind Caelus Air

Caelus Air is a fictional airline created around the idea that travel should feel comfortable, organized, and memorable.

This website introduces the brand through a simple two-page prototype. The goal is not to create a real booking system, but to show how an airline could present its identity, destinations, and purpose online.

The name "Caelus" comes from the Latin word connected to the sky. For this project, it represents the idea of travel, movement, and new experiences.

"Travel should feel simple, comfortable, and memorable."
